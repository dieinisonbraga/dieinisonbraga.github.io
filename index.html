I'm a PhD student in Data Visualization at PUC-Rio. I design and develop visualization tools to support analysts in exploring data. I am particularly interested in models and interaction techniques for data visualization.

Curriculum Vitae (CV) dbraga@inf.puc-rio.br

Short Bio

I'm following my passion for visualization through a PhD in Computer Science at the Department of Informatics of PUC-Rio, where I'm advised by Simone Barbosa and co-advised by Niklas Elmqvist. I am a member of the labs ExACTa and IDEIAS-SERG.

I am a lecturer of full-stack development as part of Software Engineering digital specialization at Central Extension Coordination (CCE) of PUC-Rio.

In industry, I've worked as web developer, data scientist, and consultant. I have worked on projects for multiple clients across industries (Oil and Gas, Digital Transformation, Legal).
